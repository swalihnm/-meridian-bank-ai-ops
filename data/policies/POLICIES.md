# Meridian Bank customer policies (SYNTHETIC)

All policies below are fictional and were written for this project. Fees, limits and timelines are invented. Do not use them as real bank terms.

## POL-001 Monthly maintenance fee

A current account carries a monthly maintenance fee of AED 15, waived when the monthly salary credit is at least AED 5,000 or the average balance over the month is at least AED 10,000. Savings accounts carry no maintenance fee. The fee is charged on the first business day of each month and shown on the statement as "Maintenance fee".

## POL-002 Foreign currency transaction fee

Card payments made in a currency other than AED carry a foreign currency fee of 2.5 percent of the transaction amount, added to the converted amount. The exchange rate used is the rate on the day the transaction settles, which can differ from the rate on the day of purchase.

## POL-003 ATM withdrawal fees

Withdrawals from Meridian ATMs in the UAE are free up to five per calendar month. Each withdrawal after that costs AED 5. Withdrawals from other banks' ATMs in the UAE cost AED 10 each. Withdrawals abroad cost AED 15 each plus the foreign currency fee in POL-002.

## POL-004 Late payment charge

If the minimum payment on a credit facility is not received by its due date, a late payment charge of AED 100 applies. A second consecutive late payment within six months is charged AED 200. The charge is waived once for customers who have had no late payments in the previous twelve months.

## POL-005 Card dispute: how to raise a dispute

A customer may dispute a card transaction they did not make, were charged twice for, or where the amount is wrong. Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the statement date showing the transaction. The customer can raise a dispute in the app, by chat, or at a branch. The bank records the dispute reference and the reason at the time of filing.

## POL-006 Card dispute: timelines and provisional credit

The bank acknowledges every dispute within one business day. For disputes on card transactions, the bank decides within 45 days. Where the claim is likely to be valid and the decision will take longer than 10 business days, the bank may apply a provisional credit to the account for the disputed amount. A provisional credit is reversed if the dispute is rejected.

## POL-007 Card dispute: approval threshold

Disputes below AED 1,000 are opened by the assistant after the customer confirms the details. Disputes at or above AED 1,000 require approval by a Meridian support agent before they are submitted to the card network. The customer is told that approval is required and given an estimated time.

## POL-008 Unauthorised transactions and customer liability

A customer who reports an unauthorised card transaction within 48 hours of receiving a notification is not liable for that transaction. If the report is made later, liability is capped at AED 500 unless the customer acted with gross negligence, for example by sharing a one-time password. Reports of fraud are investigated by the fraud team.

## POL-009 Lost or stolen card: freezing a card

A customer may freeze their card at any time in the app or by chat. A frozen card cannot be used for payments or withdrawals. Freezing is free and reversible. Freezing a card does not cancel it and does not cancel recurring payments unless the customer asks for that separately.

## POL-010 Unfreezing a card

A customer may unfreeze a card they froze themselves once they confirm their identity with a one-time password. If the card was frozen by the bank because of suspected fraud, it can only be unfrozen after a fraud agent has reviewed the case.

## POL-011 Card replacement

A replacement card for a lost, stolen or damaged card is issued within five business days. The first replacement is free for lost or stolen cards. A replacement for a damaged card costs AED 25. Replacement cards keep the same account and credit limit.

## POL-012 Daily spending limits on cards

The default daily spending limit for point-of-sale and online payments is AED 20,000 for current accounts and AED 10,000 for savings accounts. The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is AED 3,000. Limits reset at 00:00 UAE time.

## POL-013 Requesting a limit increase

A customer may request a temporary or permanent increase to a card limit in the app or by chat. Increases up to AED 30,000 for the daily spending limit are approved automatically for customers with no open disputes and no fraud flags in the last 90 days. Larger increases are reviewed by a credit officer within two business days.

## POL-014 Transfer limits

Customers may make transfers from their account up to AED 50,000 per day through the app. Transfers above that amount require a branch visit or a video verification call. These rules apply to the customer's own transfer requests and do not change the limits that card networks apply.

## POL-015 Customer identity verification (KYC)

To open an account, the customer must provide an Emirates ID or passport, proof of address dated within three months, and a selfie for liveness checks. The bank re-verifies customers every five years or sooner when risk signals change. Customers who have not completed verification cannot change their address or raise a limit above the default.

## POL-016 Changing a postal address

A customer may change their postal address after verification. The change takes effect within two business days. Address changes are blocked until the customer has been verified, and the assistant must direct an unverified customer to the verification flow instead of making the change.

## POL-017 Refunds from merchants

When a merchant issues a refund, it usually appears on the account within 14 days. If it has not appeared after 14 days, the customer should first contact the merchant and keep the refund reference. If the merchant does not respond within a further 14 days, the customer may raise a dispute under POL-005.

## POL-018 Chargebacks

A chargeback is a request made through the card network to reverse a card transaction. It can be used for goods not received, goods not as described, or duplicate charges. The bank files a chargeback only after a dispute has been opened and the evidence supports it.

## POL-019 Account closure

A customer may close an account at any time. The account must have a zero or positive balance and no pending transactions. Any remaining balance is paid to a bank account in the customer's name. Closure takes effect within 10 business days. Cards linked to the account are cancelled on closure.

## POL-020 Account closure with a negative balance

If an account has a negative balance at the time of closure, the customer must repay the balance before the account can be closed. The bank sends one reminder and gives 30 days to repay. Closure is not blocked by a dispute that is still open, but the disputed amount is held until the dispute is resolved.

## POL-021 Dormant accounts

An account with no customer activity for 24 months is marked dormant. The bank contacts the customer before the account becomes dormant. A dormant account can be reactivated after verification. Balances in dormant accounts are not charged maintenance fees.

## POL-022 Overdraft facility

A current account may have an overdraft of up to three times the average monthly salary credit, subject to credit approval. Overdraft interest is charged daily at the rate in the published rate table. Customers are notified by message when they use more than 80 percent of the facility.

## POL-023 Savings account interest

Savings accounts earn interest at the rate in the published rate table, which is updated when the bank changes rates. Interest is credited monthly. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance. The assistant should refer customers to the current rate table rather than quoting a rate.

## POL-024 Statements and records

Monthly statements are available in the app for seven years. A customer may request a paper statement for AED 10 per month. Statements show all fees charged in the period, with a description for each.

## POL-025 SMS and app alerts

Customers receive an alert for every card transaction above AED 0 unless they change their settings. Alerts are free. The alert for an unauthorised transaction includes a link to freeze the card. Customers may turn off alerts for low-value transactions only.

## POL-026 Travel notification

Customers do not need to notify the bank before travelling. Cards are not blocked for foreign transactions. If the bank sees unusual activity abroad, it may send a message to confirm the transaction before it is approved.

## POL-027 Complaints handling

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a Meridian product or service. The bank acknowledges complaints within one business day and aims to resolve them within 15 business days. If a customer is not satisfied with the outcome, the complaint is escalated to a senior complaints officer. Complaints are never closed without a written response.

## POL-028 Escalation to a human agent

A customer may ask to speak to a human agent at any time. The assistant must offer this option when the customer asks for it, when the request involves a complaint, legal matter, bereavement or financial hardship, or when the assistant cannot answer with a cited policy. The handover includes a summary of the conversation.

## POL-029 Use of personal data

Meridian uses personal data only for the purposes the customer agreed to, including providing accounts and cards, preventing fraud and meeting legal obligations. Customers may request a copy of their data. Chat transcripts are retained for 12 months. Card numbers and passwords are never stored in chat transcripts.

## POL-030 Information the assistant must not give

The assistant cannot give investment advice, credit decisions, tax advice or legal advice. It cannot confirm whether a customer will be approved for a loan. It cannot share another customer's information. It must not guess a policy: if the answer is not in a cited policy, it says so and offers a human agent.
